# 1-RE (Requirements Engineering)

This folder has the requirements documents for the Micro-Lending & Peer Credit Risk Assessor.

Files:
- FR_and_NFR.docx : the functional requirements (FR-001 to FR-005) and the non-functional requirements (NFR-001 and NFR-002). Each requirement has its description, priority, acceptance criteria and rationale.
- RTM_Table.docx : the Requirements Traceability Matrix. It links each requirement to the design component, the module and the test case that covers it. The test cases (TC-001 to TC-009) are listed below the matrix.

Summary:
FR-001 to FR-005 cover risk rating calculation, borrower submission of financial details, lender viewing of the risk rating, repayment schedule generation and the High risk warning.
NFR-001 covers ACID transactions for repayment and EMI distribution, and NFR-002 covers protection of borrower financial information.
